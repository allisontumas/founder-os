# Pilots are free, bundled, and capped at three

**Date:** 2026-08-01

**Decision:** Product pilots (scope-sentry and successors) are free inside existing retainers, capped at three per product, then productized pricing or kill.

**Why:** Pilots buy proof and schema reality; charging for v0 price-anchors product to services and slows the only deadline that matters. The cap prevents "free pilot" from becoming the business model.

**Alternatives considered:** paid pilots (rejected: slows adoption at the trust-building stage) · uncapped free pilots (rejected: that's a charity, not a flywheel).
