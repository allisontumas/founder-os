# Mara Okafor

Senior PM, 41. Competence as armor; the person everyone routes around problems *to*. Wants the project saved; needs to stop being the project's hidden float. Voice: dry, precise, kind at unexpected moments — she says "we're fine" the way other people say goodbye. Arc: from seer of everyone's constraints to confessor of her own.
