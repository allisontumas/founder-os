# Treatment (v1)

**Form:** 12-minute short, magic-realist workplace drama.

**Act I.** Mara Okafor, senior PM at a gray-carpet enterprise, runs a status meeting where every light is green and everyone is lying. That night, mid-burnout-insomnia, the gift arrives: luminous threads above every head — each person's critical path, their real one, with float rendered as slack in the line.

**Act II.** She uses it the way a PM would: quietly re-sequencing work around the truth, covering for the analyst whose thread is taut as wire, unmasking the VP whose path has months of hidden float. The project heals. Then she catches her reflection: her own thread — taut, fraying, zero float, and knotted to everyone else's.

**Act III.** The choice isn't whether to tell the truth about the project. It's whether to apply the ledger to herself. She cancels the Friday status meeting and replaces it with the one honest question. The threads fade — not because the gift leaves, but because the room no longer needs a seer. Final shot: her calendar, with float in it.
