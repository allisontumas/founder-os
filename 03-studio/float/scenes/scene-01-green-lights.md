# Scene 1 — Green Lights

INT. CONFERENCE ROOM 4B — MORNING. A wall of status slides, all green. Around the table, faces that know better. Mara runs the meeting flawlessly — and we hold on what she doesn't say. Beat: the analyst, Dev, hesitates on "on track," and Mara — kindly, deliberately — moves past it. The lie is a team sport. Button: lights off, slides still glowing green in the dark room.
