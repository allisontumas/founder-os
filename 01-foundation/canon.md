# Canon

The references that are *Jordan's* — what the brand voice and content agents may quote as touchstones. If it's not here, an agent doesn't claim it. New entries arrive via `#canon` tags in session digests.

## Poems

### "The Summer Day" — Mary Oliver
- **Why it's mine:** "your one wild and precious life" is the prioritization rubric with better line breaks.

## Scripture

### The Parable of the Talents (Matthew 25)
- **Why it's mine:** burying the talent *felt* safe and was the only move that got condemned — the founding text of bias-to-ship.

## Books

### *The Goal* — Eliyahu Goldratt
- **Why it's mine:** the constraint is the system; everything else is theater. Riverline in novel form, thirty years early.

### *Slack* — Tom DeMarco
- **Why it's mine:** float isn't waste, it's the capacity to respond. Named the studio project.

## Film & TV

### *Ted Lasso*
- **Why it's mine:** relentless good faith as an operating strategy, not naivety.
- **Key line:** "Be curious, not judgmental."

## Pop culture & internet

### "This is fine" dog
- **Why it's mine:** the patron meme of status theater — used in half the workshop decks.

## Music

### Philip Glass, *Glassworks*
- **Why it's mine:** what a well-run project sounds like — repetition with intent, drift you only notice when it resolves.
