# About

## Story so far

Jordan Rivers, 38. Twelve years running delivery — first commercial construction scheduling, then software programs at a logistics company, where the gap between "the plan" and "what anyone believed" became the obsession. Left in 2025 after one too many status meetings where every light was green and everyone knew the project was red. Founded **Riverline Advisory, LLC** in early 2026: an AI-native project-management consultancy that helps teams replace status theater with instrumented, honest delivery.

## How I think

In critical paths and float — where's the real constraint, and how much slack is everyone pretending doesn't exist. Energized by unglamorous operational truth; drained by ceremony. Writes to think; decides fast once the constraint is named.

## Skills & background

CPM scheduling, program management (PMP, long since lapsed on purpose), SQL-level data literacy, enough Python to prototype, workshop facilitation. AI-native workflows since 2024 — every Riverline engagement ships with the client's own context repo.

## What I'm building toward

A consultancy that earns the right to productize: Riverline's method becomes **scope-sentry** (software), the software feeds the writing (**The Critical Path Weekly**), and the writing feeds the consultancy. Full ledger in [aspirations.md](aspirations.md).
