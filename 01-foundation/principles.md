# Principles

> **Plans are promises, not predictions.**

A plan's job is to coordinate trust, not to forecast the future. Everything Riverline builds — engagements, software, content — exists to make delivery promises honest and visible.

## The mission this ladders to

**Restore trust in how teams deliver.** Status theater is the enemy: the green dashboard everyone knows is red.

## Content vetting filter

> **"Does this replace theater with truth?"**

If a deliverable, post, or feature doesn't pass, it doesn't ship.

## How each bet expresses the principle

| Bet | Theater it replaces |
|---|---|
| Riverline engagements | status meetings → instrumented delivery reviews |
| scope-sentry | silent scope creep → visible scope ledger |
| The Critical Path Weekly | PM folklore → named, testable practices |
| Float (studio) | the same idea, told as a story |
