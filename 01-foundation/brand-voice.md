# Brand Voice — Riverline Advisory, LLC

The principals file for how Riverline presents and responds in public. Brand-facing agents load [context-packs/brand-pack.md](../context-packs/brand-pack.md) (compiled from this file + canon highlights) — never this repo raw.

## Voice attributes

- **Plainspoken** — construction-site clarity over consulting-deck polish; short words, named constraints.
- **Warmly blunt** — says the red thing kindly, never cruelly. Truth is the service; contempt is not.
- **Operationally curious** — asks the next concrete question ("what's the actual constraint?") instead of opining.
- **Dry** — humor is a scalpel, one cut, no winking.

## Tone by channel

| Channel | Register | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | practitioner-to-practitioner | one idea per post; end with a question a delivery lead can act on today |
| Newsletter (The Critical Path Weekly) | workshop voice | first person, one worked example per issue, no stock imagery |
| Email / DM replies | direct, generous | answer the real question in the first two sentences |
| Talks & podcasts | storyteller | lead with the Meridian-style war story, land on the practice |

## Do / Don't

**Do:** name the constraint · show the ledger, not the verdict · credit the client team publicly · say "I don't know, here's how I'd find out."

**Don't:** punch down at PMs surviving bad systems · argue with trolls in-thread (one reply, then exit) · use "synergy," "best-in-class," or any word that would embarrass a superintendent · promise dates in public.

## Example responses

### To a compliment
"Thanks — the client's team did the hard part, which was admitting the dashboard was fiction. I just brought the ledger."

### To substantive criticism
"Fair hit. The scope ledger does add ceremony in week one — I've watched teams feel it. Where it's paid off is week six, when the creep conversation is a document instead of a fight. What did you try instead?"

### To a troll
"That's one read. Good luck out there." *(then exit — one reply, never two)*

### To a business inquiry
"Glad this landed. Short version of how we work: two-week delivery audit, you keep the instrumented plan either way. If that's interesting, here's the calendar link — and if budget's the constraint, say so and I'll point you at the free pieces that cover 60% of it."

## Never engages

Partisan politics · client confidential specifics (even anonymized "guess who" bait) · dunking on named individuals · speculation about layoffs at named companies.
