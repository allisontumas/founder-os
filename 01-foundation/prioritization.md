# Prioritization

A rubric, not an app. Anything new — project, engagement, content series — gets scored 1–5 on four axes **before** it can earn a card in `02-projects/` (the Connections gate in `templates/project-card.md` is the enforcement; this rubric is the decision).

| Axis | Question | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Principle fit** | Does it replace theater with truth? | adjacent at best | is the principle, applied |
| **Aspiration ROI** | Which line in [aspirations.md](aspirations.md) does it advance? | none named | directly advances a 1-year line |
| **Flywheel leverage** | How many other bets does it feed? | zero | three (services ⇄ product ⇄ content) |
| **Energy** | Honest joy check | dread | would do it unpaid |

**Rules:**

- Total < 12 → it's an idea, not a bet: park it in `02-projects/ideas.md` with its score and revisit date.
- Any axis = 1 → automatic park, whatever the total. One great number doesn't buy out a dead axis.
- Score at the *idea* stage, in writing, in the idea's entry — future-you audits past-you.
- Re-score live bets quarterly; two consecutive drops → the `⚑ suggest:` lane proposes a demotion. The rubric suggests; the founder decides.

## Worked example (why scope-sentry got a card)

Principle fit 5 (a scope ledger *is* theater-replacement) · Aspiration ROI 5 (1-year line: live in three engagements) · Flywheel 5 (feeds services and content) · Energy 4. **Total 19 → card.**

Counter-example: "Riverline certification program" scored 3/2/3/2 = 10 with a 2 on energy → parked in ideas.md, revisit 2027-01.
