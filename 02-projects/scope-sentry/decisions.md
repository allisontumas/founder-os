# Decisions

### 2026-08-01 — Pilot pricing: free, bundled into the retainer
**Why:** the pilot buys proof and schema reality, not revenue; charging for v0 would price-anchor the product to services. **Alternatives:** discounted add-on (rejected: creates a "cheap tool" anchor), paid pilot (rejected: slows the only deadline that matters). Company-level pricing philosophy graduates to `04-decisions/` when it generalizes.
