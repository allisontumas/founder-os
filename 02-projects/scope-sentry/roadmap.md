# Roadmap

1. **Pilot #1 (Hartwell)** — ledger live inside one real program; metric: team still updating it in week 6 without prompting.
2. **Pilot #2–3** — different industries, same schema; find what's universal vs Hartwell-shaped.
3. **Productize** — self-serve setup, pricing (post-pilot decision), the "permission, not detection" onboarding.
4. **The moat** — a public, anonymized corpus of how scope actually creeps; feeds the Weekly and the eventual book.
