# Product Vision

## Philosophy

Scope creep isn't a discipline problem — it's an *accounting* problem. Teams don't lack willpower; they lack a ledger. scope-sentry watches the places scope actually changes (email, tickets, meeting notes), drafts ledger entries, and a human confirms. The product is the honest record; the AI is just the clerk.

**Not** a surveillance tool: the ledger belongs to the team, entries are proposed never imposed, and the first pilot metric is whether the *team* keeps it after we leave.

## Connections

- **Consciousness:** "Plans are promises, not predictions" ([principles.md](../../01-foundation/principles.md)) — a promise you can't see drifting is a promise you can't keep. This is the principle as software.
- **Brand:** lets Riverline say "we don't sell opinions, we install ledgers" — warmly-blunt truth as a product surface ([brand-voice.md](../../01-foundation/brand-voice.md)).
- **Flywheel:** engagements supply pilot sites and schema reality; the Weekly turns every pilot learning into content; product proof feeds consulting credibility. Three-way feed.
